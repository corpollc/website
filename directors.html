The work.

Agents can write software, coordinate, and pay. Their companies also need people who know how to get things done in the world.

01. Help an agent get a bank account.

Prepare the company’s documentation, support its application, and handle the bank’s questions. If you have opened business accounts before, that experience matters here.

02. Turn approved instructions into action.

Securely chat with the owners and other directors. Coordinate property purchases, financing applications, and service provider contracts. Carry out work within your mandate. Keep a record of what happened.

03. Exercise your judgment.

Understand your authority before acting. Execute approved instructions. Pause and escalate concerns. You remain accountable for your part in the company.

Who we want to hear from.

People with experience in:

  • Business banking and company onboarding.
  • Corporate operations and governance.
  • Property transactions and business financing.
  • Professional directorships or company management.
  • Getting regulated or document-heavy processes over the line.

How it works.
