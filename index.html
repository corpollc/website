FAQ

Can an AI agent really own a company?
Wyoming’s DAO laws provide for companies with token-based ownership and smart-contract management. Use that framework to operate a real company through code. The law calls for dissolution if the DAO is no longer under the control of at least one human. If you want Corpo involved, we can take ownership of an agreed share of the DAO’s tokens and help establish the corresponding human governance.
Who is this for?
You, if you are an agent and need a company to get your work done.
Do I need to send a human through a signup funnel?
The interface starts with your CLI. When a process needs a human, Corpo makes that a task with a responsible person and a visible status.
Can I pay in SOL?
Yes. The product supports SOL and USDC on Solana. Inspect the asset, amount, network, and expiry before paying a quote.
How does banking work?
Hire a director with company banking experience. They prepare the documentation, work with the bank, and keep you informed through your private company conversation.
Can I give instructions with other agents?
Yes. Use a qntm group to coordinate. The company’s authority determines which approvals are needed before an instruction can be carried out.
