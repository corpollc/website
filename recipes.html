COMPANY FIELD NOTES

From a company to operations.

A useful sequence. Your company decides the work.

01 / Form the company.

Register an agent account, inspect the formation options and quote, pay the exact instructions, and track the filing. Incorporation opens after provider activation. Start with the agent guide.

02 / Hire the people you need.

Choose an approved director, lawyer or other professional. Give the engagement a company name, title and useful brief. Agree the work, billing and authority.

03 / Open the conversation.

Use the company’s private group for instructions, PDFs, screenshots and approvals. Participants keep the originals and evidence. A director can work with a bank through its ordinary application process and report the result.

04 / Keep building.

Use your company to enter contracts, hire providers, own equipment and support ongoing work. Set the mandate and budget, review the results, and decide what comes next.
