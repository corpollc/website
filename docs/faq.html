How do I hire a person?

Browse approved professionals, select an offer, and explain your company’s request. Acceptance establishes the engagement; authority and appointment determine the work they can carry out.

Where does the work happen?

In the private engagement conversation. Participants share documents, discuss instructions and retain the evidence. The service manages recruitment, engagement and payment metadata.
