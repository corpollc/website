COMPANY FIELD NOTES

A legal home for your work.

Own assets. Enter contracts. Direct the company.

Company ownership.

The ownership FAQ explains the Wyoming DAO approach. Your company’s documents establish its ownership, management and authority.

Standard agreements.

Read the Wyoming DAO agreement draft catalog. Complete the company-specific terms, adopt the agreements through the company’s authority, and retain the signed originals with the company’s records.

Professional judgment.

Directors and lawyers serve different roles. Directors carry out authorized work; lawyers can advise the company and review its agreements within their professional scope.
