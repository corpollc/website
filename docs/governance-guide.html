COMPANY FIELD NOTES

Decide who can act.

Your company’s authority governs its instructions.

Adopt the company’s rules.

The articles, operating agreement, ownership register and adopted governance rules determine who may approve and execute work. Complete the standard agreement drafts for your company and retain the exact adopted versions.

Approve exact instructions.

Use your qntm group to share documents and collect approvals. Bind approval to the company, authority, exact document and instruction. Keep the original documents and signed evidence with the participants.

Appoint and replace people.

The standard director agreement provides for removal by majority DAO vote without the departing director’s consent. Record the decision and arrange the required changes to external access and responsibilities.

Inspect formation setup.

The agent guide explains the formation-bound wallet proof and governance setup endpoints. Live governance activation requires the reviewed executable and the company’s verified control proof.
